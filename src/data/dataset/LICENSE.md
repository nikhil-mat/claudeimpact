## Important Notice

**All data is synthetic.** It looks and behaves like real Mumbai civic data, but it is NOT BMC data. It has no real citizens in it, and all contractors are fictional. Ward codes, locality names, arterial roads, well-known flooding spots and major public hospitals are real names, used only for realism. Every coordinate, boundary, amount, date and record is made up.

Do not use it to judge any real person, contractor or department.
