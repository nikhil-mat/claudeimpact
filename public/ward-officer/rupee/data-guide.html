🪨 Caveman Data Guide

Data live in repo/data/. 15 CSV, 2 map file. All fake. Contractor fake too.

Big rock: work_orders.csv (2,499 job). Everything connect here.

  • Each job: ward, road, problem, fix method, contractor, cost, start/end time, deadline met Y/N, fix broke within 30 day Y/N.
  • linked_complaint_ids say which complaint made job. Ids join with |. Blank = no complaint = sus.

Money rocks

  • rate_card.csv: fair price per method. Pothole patch price per sqm (area × rate). Pump and drain price per job. Normal bill within ±30% of this.
  • wards.csv: 24 ward, each with budget, how flood-y (low_lying_index 0 to 1), crew count.
  • contractors.csv: 8 fake builder.

People-yell rocks

  • complaints.csv (6,501): citizen complaints, very messy.
    • About 18% picked wrong category.
    • 7% have no ward.
    • Text in English, Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish.
    • GPS only on app complaints.
    • photo_description most honest field: 100% right.
  • complaints_sample_200.csv: small taste of big file, for quick testing.
  • social_posts.csv (650): Twitter and WhatsApp noise. Not linked to anything. Me no use.

Road rocks

  • roads.csv (291 road piece): under_dlp = Y means old builder must fix free. If BMC paid anyway, bad.
  • traffic.csv: car count and jam level per road.
  • accidents.csv: crash per road. Some caused by pothole or water.
  • facilities.csv: hospitals and schools, plus which road ambulance use.

Sky-water rocks

  • rainfall_daily.csv: rain per day at Santacruz (north) and Colaba (south).
  • tides.csv: tide height per day. Big tide is 4.5 m or more.
  • flood_hotspots.csv: 28 places that always flood, and how much rain floods them.
  • localities.csv: place name to ward and map spot. Has nicknames, including Devanagari spelling.

How rocks connect

complaint ──id──> work_order ──road_id──> road (DLP? traffic? crash? hospital?)
                      │
         contractor ──┼── method ──> rate_card (fair price?)
                      │
                   ward ──> budget

Me know these traps

  • Category citizen picked often wrong. Trust photo first, then English or helpline text.
  • Hindi, Marathi and Hinglish app text often say different problem than real one. Data generator weird. Trust it least.
  • Complaint with no GPS can find road name, but not which piece of road.
  • Fake problems hidden in data:
    • bill more than 35% over fair price
    • same job billed twice within a few day, no complaint
    • BMC paid for fix on road under DLP
  • Deadline Y/N field honest. Me checked against times.
  • Flood-y ward finish late more. Not builder fault. Me adjust for this in scorecards.
